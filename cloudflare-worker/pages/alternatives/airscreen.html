An AirScreen alternative without ads

AirScreen is one of the best-known receiver apps for Android and Android TV. If you only send from Apple devices and the ads or subscription are what bother you, ScreenDock does the AirPlay part with no ads, a one-time purchase and open-source code.

When ScreenDock is the better fit

  • You only use Apple devices to send. ScreenDock focuses on AirPlay: screen mirroring from Mac, iPhone and iPad, video from apps, and music in Apple Lossless.
  • You don't want ads on your TV or tablet. There are none, in the free or paid version.
  • You'd rather pay once. Pro is a single $9.99 purchase. Music streaming is free and unlimited even without it.
  • You want a Mac second screen. A Mac can mirror to ScreenDock or use the tablet as an extended display. See Android tablet as a Mac second screen.
  • You care what runs on your network. The code is public on GitHub, and the app has no accounts, analytics or tracking.

When to stay with AirScreen

If you also cast from Android phones, Chrome or Windows PCs, AirScreen's support for Google Cast and Miracast covers devices ScreenDock doesn't. ScreenDock is AirPlay only.

Switching

  1. Install ScreenDock from Google Play on the same phone, tablet or TV.
  2. Open it and give it a name you'll recognize, such as "Living Room TV".
  3. On your iPhone, iPad or Mac, open Screen Mirroring and pick the new name. Both apps can stay installed while you try it.
