A Mirroring360 alternative for Android

Mirroring360 AirPlay Receiver from Splashtop has been on Google Play since 2014 and is a paid app. ScreenDock is a newer AirPlay receiver you can try free before paying, with open-source code and no ads.

Facts about Mirroring360 are from its Google Play listing and Splashtop's announcement, checked 26 October 2026. Spotted something out of date? Email tarek@cressu.com.

Why people switch to ScreenDock

  • Try everything first. The free version has every feature in 10-minute sessions, and music streaming is unlimited.
  • Modern video. H.265 and up to 4K at 60 fps, shown at your screen's native resolution.
  • Mac second screen. A Mac can mirror to ScreenDock or extend its desktop onto the tablet. See Android tablet as a Mac second screen.
  • Open and private. Public source code, no accounts, no analytics.

When Mirroring360 makes sense

If you already use Splashtop's Mirroring360 tools elsewhere, for example in a classroom, staying with one vendor can be simpler.
