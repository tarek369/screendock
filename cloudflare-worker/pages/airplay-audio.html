AirPlay music to Android, including Apple Lossless

With an AirPlay receiver on your Android device, the AirPlay button in your iPhone's music controls can send songs to an Android TV, a tablet in the kitchen or an old phone plugged into speakers. Your iPhone stays the remote.

Send music

  1. Open ScreenDock on the Android device. Keep it on the same Wi-Fi as your iPhone, iPad or Mac.
  2. Start a song in Apple Music, Spotify, a podcast app or any other audio app.
  3. Tap the AirPlay icon in the app or on the Now Playing controls in Control Center, and pick the Android device. On a Mac, choose it from the Sound menu in the menu bar or the AirPlay button in the Music app.

What you get

  • Lossless when the source is. ScreenDock decodes Apple Lossless (ALAC) and AAC.
  • Now playing on screen. Song title, artist and cover art, so an Android TV turns into a music display.
  • Volume from your iPhone. The iPhone's volume buttons change the volume on the Android device.
  • Music is always free. The free version's time limit only applies to screen mirroring and video. Music streaming has no limit.

Stream Apple Music to Android TV

Install ScreenDock on the TV (see AirPlay to Android TV), then AirPlay from your iPhone as above. The TV shows the cover art and plays through its own speakers or your soundbar. It's handy when the music, playlists and account all live on your phone.

Good to know

  • Keep ScreenDock exempt from battery optimization on phones and tablets so Android doesn't stop it when the screen turns off.
  • If the device doesn't appear in the AirPlay list, see ScreenDock not showing up.
