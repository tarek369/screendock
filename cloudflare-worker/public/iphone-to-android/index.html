ScreenDock / iPhone & iPad to Android

Mirror an iPhone or iPad to an Android tablet or phone

iPhones and iPads mirror their screen with AirPlay, and Android doesn't speak AirPlay on its own. Add a receiver app to the Android device and it appears in your iPhone's Screen Mirroring list like an Apple TV would. No cable, no app on the iPhone.

What you need

  • An iPhone or iPad with Screen Mirroring (any recent iOS or iPadOS).
  • An Android phone or tablet with Android 7.0 or newer. ScreenDock is tested on Samsung Galaxy Tab and Google Pixel Tablet, and works on any Android 7.0+ tablet or phone with Google Play.
  • Both on the same Wi-Fi network. 5 GHz gives the smoothest picture.

Mirror your screen

  1. Install and open ScreenDock on the Android device. It shows Ready to receive with its name, for example "ScreenDock (Galaxy Tab S9)".
  2. Open Control Center on the iPhone or iPad. Swipe down from the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Screen Mirroring and pick the Android device. Your screen appears in a second or two, with sound.

To stop, open Control Center again and tap Stop Mirroring, or stop the receiver from the notification on the Android device.

Send a video instead of your whole screen

In YouTube or a video on a website, tap the AirPlay button and choose the Android device. The video plays there and your iPhone stays free for other things.

Good to know

  • Picture quality. ScreenDock shows the stream at the Android screen's native resolution, up to 4K at 60 fps, in H.264 or H.265.
  • Rotation. Turn your iPhone sideways for videos and games and the mirrored picture follows.
  • Fit the picture. Choose fit, fill or stretch in Settings when the shapes of the two screens differ.
  • Privacy. The stream goes straight from your iPhone to the Android device over your Wi-Fi. Nothing passes through a server. You can add a PIN or approve each new device.
  • Protected video. Netflix, Disney+ and the Apple TV app block mirroring to every third-party receiver, so those show a black screen.
  • Touch. You can't control the iPhone by touching the Android screen. Keep using the iPhone itself.

Mirroring from a Mac instead?

A Mac can do more: it can mirror or use the tablet as an extra display. See use an Android tablet as a Mac second screen.

It doesn't show up in Screen Mirroring

Check that both devices are on the same network and not on a guest Wi-Fi. More fixes in ScreenDock not showing up.
