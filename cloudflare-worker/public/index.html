What is ScreenDock?

ScreenDock is an AirPlay receiver for Android. Android can't receive AirPlay on its own; ScreenDock adds it, so an Android phone, tablet, Android TV or Google TV appears in the Screen Mirroring list on a Mac, iPhone or iPad. It mirrors the screen in H.264 or H.265 up to 4K at 60 fps, plays AirPlay music including Apple Lossless, and works over your Wi-Fi with nothing to install on the Apple side. It's free with 10-minute sessions, $9.99 once to remove the limit, has no ads, and is open source under GPLv3.

ScreenDock questions

Can Android receive AirPlay?

Not out of the box. Android has no built-in AirPlay receiver, so a Mac, iPhone or iPad won't list it. Install an AirPlay receiver app such as ScreenDock and the Android device appears in the Apple device's Screen Mirroring and AirPlay menus.

Why doesn't ScreenDock show up on my iPhone or Mac?

Make sure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network and ScreenDock says Ready to receive. Guest networks and some mesh or office networks block device discovery. The app's Troubleshooting screen walks through the common fixes, and so does this guide.

Can I watch Netflix, Disney+ or the Apple TV app?

No. Those apps protect their video with DRM, which only works with Apple-certified receivers. This is a limit of the protection, not a bug, and no third-party receiver can get around it. YouTube, browsers, photos, presentations and most other apps work.

How much delay is there?

On a good 5 GHz or Wi-Fi 6 network it's low enough for presentations, video and casual use. Keep both devices close to the router for the best result. Fast-paced gaming will feel some delay, as with any wireless mirroring.

Can I control my Mac by touching the tablet?

Not today. ScreenDock shows your screen and plays its sound. Touch control back to the Mac isn't supported.

Is my screen sent to any server?

No. Everything stays on your local network, between your Apple device and your Android. ScreenDock has no accounts, analytics or tracking. Read the privacy policy.

How does ScreenDock compare with AirScreen or Mirroring360?

ScreenDock is AirPlay-only, ad-free, open source and a one-time purchase. AirScreen also supports Google Cast and Miracast but shows ads, and Mirroring360 is a paid app. See the side-by-side comparison.

Is ScreenDock made by Apple?

No. ScreenDock is an independent open-source project under the GPLv3 license, and you can read every line of its source code on GitHub.
