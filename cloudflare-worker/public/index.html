Connected in three taps.

Nothing to install on the Apple side. ScreenDock uses the Screen Mirroring button your Mac, iPhone and iPad already have.

  1. Open ScreenDock

    Install it on your Android phone, tablet or TV and open it. It shows Ready to receive when it's listening.

  2. Open Screen Mirroring

    On iPhone or iPad, swipe down for Control Center. On a Mac, click Control Center in the menu bar.

  3. Pick your Android

    Choose ScreenDock from the list. Your screen appears in a second or two. Both devices need to be on the same Wi-Fi.

Questions

Why doesn't ScreenDock show up on my iPhone or Mac?

Make sure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network and ScreenDock says Ready to receive. Guest networks and some mesh or office networks block device discovery. The app's Troubleshooting screen walks through the common fixes.

Can I watch Netflix, Disney+ or the Apple TV app?

No. Those apps protect their video with DRM, which only works with Apple-certified receivers. This is a limit of the protection, not a bug, and no third-party receiver can get around it. YouTube, browsers, photos, presentations and most other apps work.

How much delay is there?

On a good 5 GHz or Wi-Fi 6 network it's low enough for presentations, video and casual use. Keep both devices close to the router for the best result. Fast-paced gaming will feel some delay, as with any wireless mirroring.

Can I control my Mac by touching the tablet?

Not today. ScreenDock shows your screen and plays its sound. Touch control back to the Mac isn't supported.

Is my screen sent to any server?

No. Everything stays on your local network, between your Apple device and your Android. ScreenDock has no accounts, analytics or tracking. Read the privacy policy.

Is ScreenDock made by Apple?

No. ScreenDock is an independent open-source project under the GPLv3 license, and you can read every line of its source code on GitHub.
