Which one fits you

Pick ScreenDock if

You only send from Apple devices and want no ads, a single purchase instead of a subscription, and code you can read. It mirrors a Mac at your tablet's native resolution and plays AirPlay music in Apple Lossless.

Pick AirScreen if

You also need to cast from Android phones or Windows PCs. Its support for Google Cast and Miracast covers devices that ScreenDock doesn't, and you can live with ads or pay a subscription.

Pick Mirroring360 if

You want an established receiver from Splashtop, a long-standing remote-access company, and prefer paying upfront. It has been on Google Play since 2014.

What none of them can do

No third-party AirPlay receiver can play DRM-protected video such as Netflix, Disney+ or the Apple TV app. Those apps only send protected video to Apple-certified receivers. YouTube, browsers, photos, presentations and music work in all three.
