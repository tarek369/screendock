# ScreenDock: target keywords

Status: draft, local only.

## Method and limits

No search-volume data is available from this environment, so the ranking below uses proxies:

- Title presence: AGENTS.md records that all 29 ranking competitors for "airplay receiver" and "screen mirroring" put "AirPlay" in the title. That makes "airplay" and "airplay receiver" the head terms.
- Competitor install tiers (from the Play pages, fetched 2026-10-11): AirScreen 10M+, AirMirror 5M+, AirReceiver (tier not reported in the fetch), open-source "AirPlay Receiver & Server" 1K+. High-tier competitors win broad terms, so ScreenDock targets narrower, high-intent phrases the big apps underserve.
- Our own position: absent from the top 30 for all five audit queries (AGENTS.md, 2026-10-09). Re-check after the new listing has been indexed for 2 to 4 weeks.

Competitor names are never used in any listing text (Play policy). Keyword targets come from category language, not from competitor brands.

## Avoid

- Competitor names: AirScreen, AirMirror, AirReceiver, "AirPlay Receiver & Server", and their publisher names.
- Repeating "AirPlay" in the title more than once.
- Hidden or invisible text, and keyword lists in the description.
- Claims the app does not support: touch forwarding, 120 fps, DRM mirroring.
- Trademark use that implies Apple endorsement. Keep the disclosure line in every locale.
