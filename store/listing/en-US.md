# ScreenDock: en-US

## Title
ScreenDock: AirPlay Receiver

## Short description
AirPlay receiver with no ads and a one-time Pro unlock. Mirror Apple screens.

## Full description
ScreenDock turns your Android phone, tablet or TV into an AirPlay receiver. Mirror your Mac, iPhone or iPad screen over Wi-Fi, stream music from Apple devices, and send videos from supported apps.

SCREEN MIRRORING
Mirror a Mac, iPhone or iPad to your Android device. Video is sharp H.264 and H.265 (HEVC) at up to 4K and 60 fps, shown at your display's native resolution. Audio is AAC or Apple Lossless (ALAC).

Use an Android tablet or monitor as a second display for your Mac, with no cables or adapters. Or mirror your iPhone or iPad to a spare Android device and use it as a portable screen.

AIRPLAY AUDIO
Stream music from your iPhone, iPad or Mac, including lossless ALAC. Song title, artist and cover art appear on screen while you listen.

AIRPLAY VIDEO
Send YouTube and web videos from Apple devices to ScreenDock with the AirPlay button. Playback runs on your Android device.

ANDROID TV AND GOOGLE TV
Install ScreenDock on Android TV or Google TV and it appears as an AirPlay receiver in the Apple device's menu.

HIGHLIGHTS
- Screen mirroring from macOS, iOS and iPadOS
- AirPlay audio receiver with now-playing info and cover art
- AirPlay video from supported apps and websites
- Android TV and Google TV support
- Fit, fill or stretch the picture to your screen
- Optional PIN, and "ask before allowing" for each new sender
- Optional start at boot
- Stop the receiver from the status notification
- Open source, built on the UxPlay core

FREE AND PRO
Free: 10 minutes of mirroring or video, then a 30-minute break. Music streaming is always free. ScreenDock Pro is a single one-time purchase with no subscription and no ads.

REQUIREMENTS
- Sender and receiver on the same Wi-Fi network (5 GHz recommended for lowest latency)
- An Apple device with Screen Mirroring or AirPlay support

PLEASE NOTE
- DRM-protected content (Netflix, Disney+, Apple TV+ and similar) cannot be mirrored. This is a technical limitation of the protection, not a bug.
